How to make a QR code in 3 steps

This free QR code generator runs in your browser. Paste a link, pick a style, and download a printable code — no account and nothing uploaded to a server.

  1. Add your content

    Use a website URL, Google review link, Wi-Fi network, WhatsApp number, Instagram username, email, phone number, or plain text.

  2. Customize the look

    Choose dots, hearts, stars, or other shapes, then set a brand color. Square and circle styles scan most reliably.

  3. Download and share

    Save PNG for social posts, SVG for print, JPG for smaller files, or WebP for websites. Copy the image if you want to paste it into a chat or doc.

QR code generator FAQ

How do I create a QR code for a website?

Choose Website URL, paste the full link including https://, then download the QR code. When someone scans it, their camera opens that page.

Is this QR code generator free?

Yes. You can create and download QR codes at no cost, with no account and no watermark.

Can I download a QR code as PNG, JPG, WebP, or SVG?

Yes. PNG is best for sharing, JPG for smaller files, WebP for modern web use, and SVG for print or logos because it stays sharp at any size.

How do I make a heart or star QR code?

Open the Style tab and choose Heart or Star for the dots, marker border, or marker center. High error correction is used so decorative shapes still scan.

Can I make a Wi-Fi QR code?

Yes. Choose Wi-Fi login, enter the network name and password, then download. Guests can join by scanning instead of typing the password.

Do I need to sign up? Will my QR code expire?

No signup is required. Codes are static and generated in your browser, so they do not expire and keep working as long as the destination still exists.

Can I print this QR code on flyers, menus, or business cards?

Yes. Download SVG or a large PNG for print. Leave a quiet white margin around the code and test a scan before you print a large batch.

How do I make a QR code for Instagram, WhatsApp, or Google reviews?

Pick Instagram, WhatsApp, or Google Maps / reviews in the content menu, enter your username, number, or review link, then download.
